Mergers & Acquisitions

Decisive capital for complex opportunities.

Cook Services Company pursues complex control transactions, strategic acquisitions, and special-situations opportunities involving businesses undergoing financial, operational, ownership, or governance pressure.

We focus on situations where conventional buyers may be unwilling or unable to act — identifying enterprises with impaired capital structures, underutilized assets, ownership dislocation, management challenges, or unrealized strategic value.
Our Approach

Control. Capital. Transformation.

CSC evaluates opportunities to acquire, recapitalize, restructure, reposition, or consolidate businesses through disciplined transaction structures and hands-on ownership.

01 / SPECIAL SITUATIONS

Distressed & Complex Opportunities

Businesses experiencing financial distress, ownership instability, succession challenges, capital constraints, or operational underperformance can create compelling control opportunities.

02 / LEVERAGED ACQUISITIONS

Leveraged Buyouts

Where appropriate, acquisitions may combine sponsor capital, transaction financing, acquired-company cash flow, and other lawful financing sources to support an efficient transfer of control.

03 / CONTROL TRANSACTIONS

Contested & Unsolicited Opportunities

Not every transaction begins through a traditional sale process. CSC evaluates lawful negotiated and contested pathways to corporate control where strategic conviction warrants action.
